WHO WE ARE

A Practitioner-Led
Technology Firm

Best Entrust, LLC is a Maryland-based technology consulting and workforce training firm founded in 2006. We are led by Andrew H. Mangle, Ph.D., a practitioner-educator who brings deep expertise across cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, data analytics, and digital transformation.

Our approach is grounded in real-world application. Every training program we design is co-developed with employer partners, benchmarked against labor market data, and delivered using tools that participants will use on day one of employment.

We have served federal agencies, higher education institutions, nonprofits, and regional small businesses. Our engagements range from strategic technology advisory to hands-on workforce training cohorts, all anchored in measurable outcomes.

Prince George's County is our home. We are invested in building the technology talent pipeline that keeps Maryland competitive in the AI era.
