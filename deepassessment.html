The problem this solves

Self-assessments rate intentions. Risk accumulates in operations.

Your organization has an AI Readiness Index score — a self-reported snapshot. Useful, but self-assessments have a known failure mode: organizations rate themselves on intentions, while risk accumulates in operations. The Deep Assessment replaces self-report with evidence: interviews, document review, and observation across all seven dimensions — producing a score your leadership can defend to a board, a regulator, or themselves.

What happens over the two weeks
WK 1Evidence

Activity

Structured interviews with 6–10 stakeholders across leadership, IT/security, operations, and frontline teams. Review of policies, data architecture documentation, vendor contracts, and current AI tool usage.

You receive

Interim findings memo — including anything urgent enough that it shouldn't wait for the final report.

WK 2Analysis & Roadmap

Activity

Evidence-based rescore across all seven dimensions. Gap analysis against sector expectations. Prioritized 12-month roadmap with owners, sequence, and budget logic.

You receive

Written report (15–25 pages), evidence-based Readiness Index score, and a 90-minute executive briefing session.
